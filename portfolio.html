IN Realty

An independent real estate brokerage serving the Research Triangle region of North Carolina. IN Realty delivers a more personal, service-driven approach to buying and selling, supported by a knowledgeable team and in-house expertise across every stage of the process.

As a new entrant in a competitive real estate market, IN Realty needed a brand that could establish credibility quickly while differentiating itself from larger, more impersonal brokerages.

Industry

Real Estate

Scope

  • Identity Design
  • Print & Collateral
  • Web Design

Challenge

Without an existing brand foundation, the identity needed to do more than look polished—it had to build trust, communicate expertise, and create a distinct presence from day one across both print and digital touchpoints.

Approach

The goal was to develop a refined, flexible identity system that balances professionalism with personality—positioning IN Realty as a modern, boutique alternative while maintaining clarity and consistency across all applications.

Identity Design

The logotype is built on the foundation of Brandon, introducing subtle customization for a more distinctive presence. A stylized rooftop form with an arched window replaces the “A” in “Realty,” creating a memorable visual cue tied to residential property and reinforcing immediate brand recognition.

Print & Collateral

A cohesive print system extends the identity across business cards, stationery, and marketing materials. Premium typography, a restrained palette of tonal blues, and editorial-style photography establish trust and refinement, while a custom illustrative pattern introduces energy and movement.

A targeted direct mail campaign was developed to support local outreach, translating the identity into a format designed for visibility and engagement. Clear messaging and consistent visuals help establish recognition within the community.

Web Design

The website translates the identity into a clean, intuitive digital experience. High-fidelity mockups define structure, hierarchy, and tone, with a focus on navigation and footer systems to create a cohesive and user-friendly platform.

Outcome

The result is a cohesive, elevated brand system that establishes IN Realty as a credible and differentiated presence from launch—supporting growth across print, digital, and local outreach.

Hinson's Printing

A full-service print company with over 40 years of experience.

They bring brands to life across print and physical touchpoints—from offset printing to large-format graphics, apparel, and promotional products.

Industry

Print & Production

Services

  • Identity Refresh
  • Print & Promotional System
  • Environmental Application

Overview

Hinson’s Printing evolved into a modern, full-service provider, but its identity remained rooted in its past—creating a disconnect between perception and capability.

Challenge

The existing identity lacked the flexibility and clarity needed to represent the breadth of Hinson’s services. It felt dated, limiting how the brand showed up across print, promotional materials, and real-world applications.

Approach

The goal was to create a refined, adaptable identity system that could scale across a wide range of outputs—from small-format print to large environmental applications—while maintaining clarity and consistency.

Identity Refresh

The refreshed identity introduces a more contemporary, versatile mark designed for clarity and flexibility across formats. Simplified forms and balanced proportions ensure the logo performs consistently, from business cards to large-scale applications.

Print & Promotional System

A cohesive suite of business cards and promotional materials designed to extend the refreshed identity across print, ensuring consistency and clarity at every touchpoint.

Environmental Application

A scalable vehicle wrap system designed to carry the refreshed identity into the physical environment—transforming fleet vehicles into bold, mobile brand assets while maintaining clarity and legibility in motion.

Outcome

The result is a cohesive, modern identity system that aligns with Hinson’s expanded capabilities—strengthening brand presence across print, promotional materials, and the physical environment.
