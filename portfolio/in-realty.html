IN Realty

An independent real estate brokerage serving the Research Triangle region of North Carolina. IN Realty delivers a more personal, service-driven approach to buying and selling, supported by a knowledgeable team and in-house expertise across every stage of the process.

As a new entrant in a competitive real estate market, IN Realty needed a brand that could establish credibility quickly while differentiating itself from larger, more impersonal brokerages.

Industry

Real Estate

Scope

  • Identity Design
  • Print & Collateral
  • Web Design

Challenge

Without an existing brand foundation, the identity needed to do more than look polished—it had to build trust, communicate expertise, and create a distinct presence from day one across both print and digital touchpoints.

Approach

The goal was to develop a refined, flexible identity system that balances professionalism with personality—positioning IN Realty as a modern, boutique alternative while maintaining clarity and consistency across all applications.

Identity Design

The logotype is built on the foundation of Brandon, introducing subtle customization for a more distinctive presence. A stylized rooftop form with an arched window replaces the “A” in “Realty,” creating a memorable visual cue tied to residential property and reinforcing immediate brand recognition.

Print & Collateral

A cohesive print system extends the identity across business cards, stationery, and marketing materials. Premium typography, a restrained palette of tonal blues, and editorial-style photography establish trust and refinement, while a custom illustrative pattern introduces energy and movement.

A targeted direct mail campaign was developed to support local outreach, translating the identity into a format designed for visibility and engagement.

Web Design

The website translates the identity into a clean, intuitive digital experience.

Outcome

The result is a cohesive, elevated brand system that establishes IN Realty as a credible and differentiated presence from launch—supporting growth across print, digital, and local outreach.
