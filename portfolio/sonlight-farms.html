Sonlight Farms

Sonlight Farms is an agritourism destination in Kenly, North Carolina, offering seasonal attractions, fresh produce, and family-friendly experiences that bring the local community together throughout the year.

To support its growing presence, Sonlight Farms needed a distinctive identity that reflected its welcoming personality while remaining flexible across signage, merchandise, and marketing materials.

Industry

Agritourism

Scope

  • Logo Design

Challenge

The identity needed to capture the warmth and authenticity of the farm while standing out in a competitive agritourism market. It had to appeal to families and seasonal visitors while remaining recognizable across both large-scale applications and smaller branded materials.

Approach

The solution centered around a custom illustrated mark paired with a handcrafted wordmark lockup. Together, they create a memorable identity that feels approachable, timeless, and rooted in the character of the farm, while providing the flexibility needed for a wide range of applications.

Logo Design

The logo combines a custom illustration with a bespoke wordmark lockup to create an identity that is both distinctive and inviting. The illustration reflects the farm's character and seasonal appeal, while the typography complements it with a handcrafted feel that reinforces authenticity and approachability.

Identity Details

The mark was designed to maintain clarity and recognition across a variety of uses, from roadside signage and apparel to printed materials and digital platforms.

Together, the illustration and custom wordmark establish a memorable visual identity that reflects the welcoming experience visitors can expect at Sonlight Farms.

Outcome

The finished identity gives Sonlight Farms a memorable and versatile brand presence that celebrates its unique character while supporting recognition across seasonal promotions, on-site experiences, and future brand applications.
